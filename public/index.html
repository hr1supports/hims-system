Onboarding Overview

HR1 Onboarding Priorities

  • Review candidate details and progress in Applications.
  • Update each candidate's status as they move through screening and interviews.
  • Mark an accepted candidate Hired. They appear in Onboarding automatically, with a checklist to complete.

Onboarding Handoff

  • Applicants do not receive HIMS accounts while their applications are under review.
  • After HR marks an applicant Hired, Super Admin creates their restricted trainee account.
  • After training, Super Admin promotes that same account to Employee.

What HR Staff can do

  • Encode leave and overtime for employees.
  • Encode claims with receipts. An HR Officer approves or rejects them.
  • Add applicants and move them through Applied, Screening and Interview. The HR Officer makes the final Hired or Rejected decision.
  • Schedule interviews and record their results.
  • Work through each new hire's onboarding checklist (documents, medical, contract, orientation, ID and uniform).
  • Prepare employee self-service requests after an HR Officer approves them, then mark them Completed.
  • View the employee directory and employee records.

What HR Officers can do

  • Approve or reject claims, overtime and employee self-service requests.
  • Make the final hiring decision (Hired or Rejected) for applicants who finished their interview.

  • Publish or decline hire orders from HR3 so they appear on the careers page.
  • View HR analytics, the scorecard, employee records and the audit log, and export password-protected reports.
